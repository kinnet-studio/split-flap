All examples

Ripple

Click anywhere: the next colour spreads out from that unit, each unit starting later the further away it is.

Game of Life

Two flaps per unit, so a cell flips once when it is born or dies. Click a unit to toggle its cell.

Plasma

Overlapping waves on a drum of twelve hues. Units take the shorter way round the drum, so the bands flow.

Equalizer

Each unit flips between off and on; the painter colours lit units by row.

Clock

A 3 × 5 pixel font. Each second only the pixels that change flip.
